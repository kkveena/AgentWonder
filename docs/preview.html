Operating Model Notes

Squad Lead

Accountable for overall squad success, progress visibility, roadblock escalation, and keeping the squad aligned to outcomes.

Governance

Ensures solid design principles, edge-case thinking, guardrails, policy adherence, and compliance with Citi standards.

Product + Governance

Own show-and-tell discipline, keep the team grounded in business needs, and validate that design remains explainable and usable.

MVP Quality Bar

MVP does not mean poor design. The design should be thorough, open, and ready to expand as the platform matures.

AI Integration Engineer

A cross-squad role focused on end-to-end integration, stakeholder alignment, platform quality, and future-proofing.

Expected Responsibilities

  • Interface with business, product, engineering, DS, UI, and governance stakeholders.
  • Ensure data quality and integration readiness across tools, workflows, policies, prompts, and platform services.
  • Understand current platform state and maintain a clear vision of how the platform integrates as one unit.
  • Keep ears open for future requirements and help steer or change direction when needed for platform success.
